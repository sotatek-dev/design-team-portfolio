Brand overview

WedMoment is an online wedding platform designed to help couples plan, present and share their special day in one beautifully connected experience. From invitations and schedules to stories, galleries and guest details, everything lives in one elegant digital space.

The website was shaped around emotion, simplicity and trust. A refined visual language, intuitive navigation and thoughtful content hierarchy make wedding information easy to explore while preserving the warmth and personality of each couple’s story.

  • UX research and user journeys
  • UI design and visual direction
  • Responsive wedding experience

Turning every wedding story into a beautiful,
seamless digital experience.

01. Discovery & Journey

We mapped the needs of couples and guests to simplify key journeys such as invitations, event details, schedules, locations and shared memories.

02. Experience Design

A flexible UX system brings essential wedding information together in a clear, intuitive flow while keeping the experience personal and emotionally engaging.

03. Visual Direction

Elegant typography, soft imagery and generous spacing create a timeless visual language that feels romantic, calm and effortlessly premium.
